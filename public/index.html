The visa desk

Visa refusals usually come down to one missing or weak document. Pick a country to see what you'll need. We prepare the file, check it against the latest rules, and sit a mock interview with you.

UK Student visa

  • Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies (CAS) from your university
  • Proof of funds for tuition and living costs, held for 28 consecutive days
  • English test result your university accepts
  • Tuberculosis test certificate from an approved clinic
  • Immigration Health Surcharge paid online
  • Biometrics appointment at the visa application centre

Our UK files: 99% approved in 2025.

Canada study permit

  • Letter of acceptance from a designated learning institution
  • Provincial attestation letter, where your programme needs one
  • Proof of funds, including a GIC with a participating bank
  • Study plan explaining why this course and why Canada
  • Medical exam with an IRCC panel physician
  • Biometrics at the visa application centre

Canada's rules change often. We re-check every file the week we submit it.

Australia student visa (subclass 500)

  • Confirmation of Enrolment (CoE) from your university
  • Answers for the Genuine Student requirement
  • Overseas Student Health Cover for your whole stay
  • Evidence of funds for tuition, living and travel
  • English test result
  • Health examination, if asked

We draft your Genuine Student answers with you, not for you.

Ireland study visa

  • Letter of offer from an Irish university
  • Receipt for tuition fees paid
  • Bank statements showing the required living funds
  • Private medical insurance
  • English test result
  • After arrival: Irish Residence Permit registration

We book your residence permit appointment before you land.

Germany national visa for study

  • Admission letter from a German university
  • APS certificate verifying your Indian academic documents
  • Blocked account with the amount required for the year
  • Health insurance valid in Germany
  • Proof of English or German, as your course requires
  • Motivation letter and CV

Apply for APS early. It's the step that delays most German applications.

Fees and straight answers

Questions parents ask us in the first ten minutes.

Is the first counselling session really free?

Yes. It's a 45-minute call or office visit with a senior counsellor. You leave with a rough shortlist and a budget, whether or not you sign up.

How do you make money?

Some universities pay us a fee when a student enrols. We tell you which universities on your shortlist those are, and we never shortlist a course just because of it. Our visa-only service has a flat fee, listed below.

What does visa-only help cost?

₹18,000 for a complete student visa file, including document review, mock interview and appointment booking. Government visa fees are paid separately.

Can you guarantee a visa?

No one honestly can. The decision belongs to the embassy. What we guarantee is a complete, checked file and a straight opinion on your chances before you pay the visa fee.

My marks aren't great. Is it worth calling?

Yes. A lot of good universities look at your whole profile. We'll tell you where you're likely to get in, and what would improve your chances.
